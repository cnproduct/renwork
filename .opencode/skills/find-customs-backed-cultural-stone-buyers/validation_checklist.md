# Quality & Audit Validation Checklist — Tianya Cultural Stone

## A. Pre-Discovery Requirements
- [ ] Tianya Stone Search Brief is complete ([references/tianya-stone-profile.md](references/tianya-stone-profile.md)).
- [ ] Core product lines (Cultural Stone Veneer, Ledger Panels, Natural Limestone, Granite Pavers) and HS codes (`6810.19`, `6802.91-99`, `6803.00`) are mapped.
- [ ] Proof assets (ASTM C97/C170/C67 reports, CE certification, HD digital catalog, FCL crate video, Physical sample box) are documented.
- [ ] Hard disqualifiers (Logistics forwarders, retail consumers, machinery vendors) are specified before search starts.

## B. Pilot 10 Scale Gate Check
- [ ] Ten representative candidate accounts are fully populated.
- [ ] Independent Sales Validation pass is executed.
- [ ] At least **6 of 10** accounts are graded **R1 or R2**.
- [ ] At least **3 of 10** have verified **E1/E2** customs evidence or an active project/procurement trigger.
- [ ] No more than **2 of 10** are hard exclusions (RX).
- [ ] False-positive patterns and revised search terms are logged.

## C. Customs & Trade Evidence Audit
- [ ] Legal buyer entity and operating site are resolved.
- [ ] Shipment/sample date falls inside `run_date - 6 months` window.
- [ ] China origin scope is explicitly recorded as Mainland China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, or Mixed.
- [ ] Trade evidence URL opens directly to verifiable records.
- [ ] Freight forwarders, NVOCCs, 3PL logistics, and FBA warehouses are excluded.

## D. Contact & Decision-Maker Audit
- [ ] Decision-maker profile is verified for current employer and title.
- [ ] Relevance to stone procurement, sourcing, category management, estimation, or supply chain is confirmed.
- [ ] C1/C2/C3/C0 grade is assigned with timestamp.
- [ ] No personal emails or phones were hallucinated or inferred from domain patterns.

## E. Sales Validation & Action Grading
- [ ] Procurement model (Direct Importer, Masonry Dealer, Project Contractor, OEM Brand Owner) is diagnosed.
- [ ] Active buying trigger or China cost-down opening is identified.
- [ ] R1/R2/R3/RX grade is assigned independently from discovery.
- [ ] Every R2/R3 row has an explicit manual-review question.
- [ ] Every RX row has an explicit exclusion reason.
- [ ] Prohibited claims list is attached to the account record.

## F. Outreach & Delivery Verification
- [ ] Day 0 email pitch uses a verified observation, specific Tianya stone line, and complimentary sample kit offer.
- [ ] Day 7 follow-up includes a new micro-value asset (ASTM summary, CAD textures, crate video).
- [ ] 17-sheet master Excel workbook is fully populated without syntax errors or unverified placeholders.
- [ ] Generated `.xlsx` workbook and `.md` report are saved to the Artifact Directory AND backed up to the current project workspace directory.
