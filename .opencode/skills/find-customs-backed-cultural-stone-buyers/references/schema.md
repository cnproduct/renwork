# Field Dictionary & Workbook Schema — Tianya Cultural Stone Buyer Pipeline

## 1. Required Excel Workbook Sheets (17 Mandatory Sheets)

1. `Executive Summary` — Project overview, total buyers discovered, pilot pass rate, scale gate status, score distribution.
2. `Tianya Stone Search Brief` — Tianya product matrix, ASTM/CE compliance, HS codes, MOQ/lead times, approved vs. prohibited claims.
3. `Buyer Archetypes and Market Hypotheses` — ICP definitions for stone importers, masonry dealers, contractors, and OEMs.
4. `Pilot 10 Validation` — Pilot 10 audit results, false positive analysis, scale gate pass/fail decision.
5. `Global Candidate Master` — Deduplicated master repository of all evaluated companies.
6. `Strict Six-Month China-Related Purchases` — E1 strict customs records matching China origin, run-date-minus-6-months window, and stone product context.
7. `Trade Evidence and Gaps` — All trade evidence rows (E1, E2, E3) with source links and limitation notes.
8. `Contacts LinkedIn XING and Job Sources` — Verified decision-makers, titles, C-grades, profile URLs, and job posting signals.
9. `Sales Validation and Action Grades` — Independent sales pass results, R-grades (R1, R2, R3, RX), validation questions, and 100-point scores.
10. `Company and Personal Social Links` — Official company website, LinkedIn company page, and verified social routes.
11. `Multi-Channel Outreach Plan` — Day 0 outreach angles, recommended channels, proof assets, and proposed sample kit offers.
12. `Day-7 Follow-Up Queue` — Day 7 value-add assets (ASTM reports, packing videos, CAD textures) and follow-up schedules.
13. `Exclusions and Manual Review` — RX excluded accounts and R2/R3 manual review reasons.
14. `Country Job-Board Matrix` — Search terms, roles, and job portals used per target country.
15. `Search and Verification Log` — Chronological record of search queries, trade databases, verification dates, and operator notes.
16. `Field Dictionary and Scoring` — Definition of every column, evidence grade, contact grade, R-grade, and scoring weights.
17. `CRM Feedback and Learning Log` — Post-outreach response logging, learned buyer feedback, and iterative ICP refinement.

---

## 2. Mandatory Data Columns (Field Dictionary)

- `Company Name` (Parent Group & Legal Buyer Entity)
- `Country / State / City`
- `Official Website & Domain Suffix`
- `Buyer Archetype` (Stone Importer, Masonry Dealer, Facade Contractor, Hardscape Specialist, Stone OEM)
- `Entity Resolution Status` (Resolved / Unresolved)
- `Procurement Model` (Direct FCL Importer, Indirect Dealer, Project Contractor, OEM Brand Owner)
- `Trade Direction` (Import / Export / Domestic)
- `Shipment Date` (YYYY-MM-DD inside 6-month window)
- `Stone Product Description`
- `HS Code` (e.g. 6810.19, 6802.91, 6803.00)
- `Supplier / Shipper Name`
- `Origin` (Mainland China / Hong Kong / Taiwan / Mixed)
- `Customs Source & Openable URL`
- `Evidence Grade` (E1 / E2 / E3 / U0)
- `Decision-Maker Name & Title` (e.g. Procurement Director, Stone Category Manager)
- `LinkedIn / Profile URL`
- `Contact Grade` (C1 / C2 / C3 / C0)
- `Public Business Email & Purpose Tag` (e.g. B2B Sales / Procurement)
- `Email Source URL & Verification Basis` (Mandatory verifiable URL or official registry record)
- `Public Phone & Official Contact Page URL` (Mandatory verifiable URL or official registry record)
- `Phone Source URL & Verification Basis` (Mandatory verifiable URL or official registry record)
- `Current Trigger Event` (Catalog Refresh, Spring Stocking, Project Specification, Active Buyer Hiring)
- `Sales Action Grade` (R1 / R2 / R3 / RX)
- `Manual Review / Exclusion Reason`
- `First-Touch Angle & Recommended Proof Asset`
- `100-Point Score & Customer Grade` (A+, A, B, C, RX)
- `Verification Date` (Mandatory timestamp)
