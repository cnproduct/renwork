# Sales Validation Rubric — Cultural Stone & Building Stone Buyers

Use this rubric during the independent second pass. The Sales Validation Agent must challenge discovery assumptions and evaluate true commercial procurement fit.

---

## 1. Procurement Model Diagnosis

Classify each candidate account into one of five verified models:

1. **Direct Stone Importer / Stocking Wholesaler:** The company owns stone yards or distribution warehouses, imports 20ft FCL containers directly, and distributes to regional dealers/contractors. Highest priority target.
2. **Building Material & Masonry Dealer:** The company stocks wall cladding, ledger panels, and pavers alongside brick/cements. May import directly or buy from regional importers. High priority if direct importing capability is verified.
3. **Project / Facade Engineering Contractor:** The company bids on commercial or luxury residential construction projects, specifying and purchasing stone cladding panels directly for project sites.
4. **Hardscape & Landscape Contractor:** The company designs and builds outdoor living spaces, plazas, and pool surrounds, purchasing granite pavers, limestone coping, and ledger stone.
5. **Private Label Stone Brand / OEM Partner:** Overseas stone brand outsourcing cultural stone veneer or ledger panel manufacturing to Chinese factories under OEM terms.

---

## 2. Opportunity Triggers (采购开单触发因素)

### Strong Triggers (高价值开单信号)
- **Catalog Refresh / Pattern Expansion:** Overseas buyer introducing new ledger stone textures or limestone colorways.
- **Spring/Summer Building Season Stocking:** Pre-season stocking of exterior wall cladding and pavers (typically Jan–April in Northern Hemisphere).
- **Cost-Down / Supply Chain Diversification:** High freight rates or price increases from domestic/European stone suppliers driving search for China factory direct supply.
- **New Project Bidding / Specification:** Major hotel, resort, or residential community facade contract award requiring large-volume exterior stone cladding.
- **Active Procurement Hiring:** Job postings for Stone Category Manager, Building Material Buyer, Import Logistics Coordinator.

### Weak Signals (弱信号 / 需复核)
- Generic company growth or social media post about stone architecture.
- Historic customs shipment older than 12 months without recent activity.
- General corporate contact email (`info@domain.com`) without verified decision-maker.

---

## 3. Hard Disqualifiers (硬性排除对象)

- Freight forwarders, NVOCCs, customs brokers, 3PL logistics hubs, FBA warehouses.
- Individual homeowners or retail DIY consumers seeking small retail quantities (< 1 FCL).
- Pure stone machinery, diamond tool, or abrasive suppliers.
- Direct Chinese domestic trading companies re-selling without overseas import entity.
- Closed or dissolved business entities.

---

## 4. R-Grade Decision Framework (R1 / R2 / R3 / RX)

### R1 — Develop Now (即刻开发)
- **Criteria:** Strong ICP fit (Importer, Wholesaler, Contractor, OEM); active FCL import history; verified procurement decision-maker (C1/C2); clear trigger or cost-down opening; exact Tianya proof asset (ASTM report / HD catalog / Sample kit offer) ready.

### R2 — Validate Then Develop (验证后开发)
- **Criteria:** Strong technical/product fit, but one commercial element needs focused validation:
  - Is the buyer importing FCL directly or purchasing through a local master distributor?
  - Does the project contractor have spec authority or is the stone pre-specified by the architect?
  - Is the annual import volume sufficient for Tianya's FCL MOQ?

### R3 — Nurture / Observe (长期观察)
- **Criteria:** Relevant stone company, but current quarry/supplier contract is locked, project timeline is distant, or key decision-maker is unconfirmed.

### RX — Exclude (排除)
- **Criteria:** Hard disqualifier met (logistics entity, retail consumer, machinery vendor, false entity). Must state exact exclusion reason.

---

## 5. 100-Point Sales Scoring System

| Scoring Component | Max Points | Evaluation Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Product / Application Fit** | 20 | Cultural stone veneer, ledger panels, limestone, or granite pavers match Tianya's core lines (20). Partial match (12). Low match (5). |
| **Procurement Model & Openness** | 20 | Direct FCL importer/wholesaler open to China factory direct OEM (20). Indirect buyer (10). Locked exclusive supplier (0). |
| **Customs & Trade Evidence** | 20 | E1 strict 6-month China proof (20). E2 credible trade evidence (14). E3 demand signal (8). U0 (0). |
| **Current Trigger & Timing** | 15 | Active project bid, catalog refresh, spring stocking, active buyer hiring (15). Moderate signal (8). No trigger (0). |
| **Decision-Maker Reachability** | 15 | C1 verified buyer title (15). C2 needs title confirmation (10). C3 referral path (5). C0 no contact (0). |
| **Public Channel Quality** | 10 | Verified corporate email / procurement route (10). General email only (5). No channel (0). |

**Grade Conversion:** A+ (90–100), A (82–89), B (72–81), C (< 72), RX (Excluded).
