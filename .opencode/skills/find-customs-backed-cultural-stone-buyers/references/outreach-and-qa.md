## 2. Sample Outreach Sequences & Touchpoints

### Day 0 Package (Initial Touchpoint)
- **Subject Line:** Specific & Product-Focused (e.g. `Natural Slate Ledger Stone & Limestone Cladding — Direct Factory Specs for [Company Name]`)
- **Opening:** One verified factual observation about the prospect (e.g., `Noticed [Company Name]'s recent expansion in natural stone wall cladding series...`).
- **Value Proposition:** Introduce Fujian Tianya's quarry-direct manufacturing in Shuitou, factory-direct FCL pricing, and strict shade sorting.
- **Proof Asset:** Attach high-res product spec sheet or link to digital 3D texture catalog.
- **Call to Action:** Offer a complimentary curated **Physical Sample Swatch Box** (4–6 stone samples) delivered via DHL/FedEx for technical evaluation.

### Day 7 Follow-Up (Value-Add Touchpoint)
- Do NOT send generic "Just checking in" emails.
- Provide a new micro-value asset:
  - Container load packing demonstration video (ISPM-15 crate protection);
  - ASTM C97 water absorption & ASTM C170 compressive strength test report comparison;
  - CAD / SketchUp texture maps for architectural rendering.

## 3. Physical Sample Kit Strategy (样品盒透传策略)

For R1 accounts (Importers, Wholesalers, Masonry Dealers), sending a physical sample kit is the highest converting sales touchpoint in the stone industry:
- **Sample Box Contents:** 4 to 6 curated stone veneer swatches (10x15cm cut samples showing texture, color depth, corner pieces, and backing).
- **Inclusions:** Printed ASTM test summary card, factory virtual tour QR code, personalized cover letter from Tianya's international sales engineering team.
- **Dispatch Rule:** Verify full shipping address, recipient phone number, and express courier account before dispatch.

---

## 4. Prohibited & High-Risk Messaging Claims

- Do NOT state pin-point price matches without seeing exact dimensional specifications and stone grade.
- Do NOT claim zero breakage during sea freight; state Tianya's standard reinforced crate packaging and standard 2% transit tolerance.
- Do NOT send unsolicited bulk emails without an explicit unsubscribe route and legitimate B2B business rationale.
