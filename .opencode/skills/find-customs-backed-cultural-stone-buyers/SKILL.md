# Find Customs-Backed Cultural Stone Buyers

Build an auditable, sales-validated overseas buyer pipeline for Fujian Tianya Cultural Stone Co., Ltd. (福建天涯文化石有限公司, www.diytale.com / tystoneveneer.com), not merely a list of stone-related companies. Keep customs evidence, legal entity identity, procurement decision-maker identity, and commercial priority as strictly separate verification layers.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Never invent a company, shipment, date, origin, product description, HS code, volume/weight, BOL, supplier, contact, title, LinkedIn/XING profile, email, phone, certification, capacity, ASTM test result, or purchase need.
2. Never use dummy or placeholder telephone number patterns (e.g. `555`, `8800`, `1234`, `0000`). If an exact phone number is not independently verified from an official source, mark it strictly as `未公开核验`.
3. Leave unsupported fields blank or mark them `未公开核验` / `待复核`. Never fill a requested count with synthetic or unverified rows.
4. **Strict Binary File Link Prohibition**: NEVER output a direct markdown link pointing to a binary file (such as `.xlsx`, `.zip`, `.pdf`, `.png`, `.jpg`). Clicking direct binary links in the Antigravity UI opens them in editor tabs as raw/garbled plain text. Instead, always output a markdown link pointing to the **parent directory/folder** of the file (e.g. `📁 [在 Finder 中查看所在文件夹](file:///absolute/path/to/parent/directory/)`) and instruct the user to double-click the file in the Finder window revealed automatically by the `open -R` command.
5. Never infer a personal email from a name, domain, or naming pattern. Record only a publicly displayed address and its exact source, purpose, and verification date.
6. Label search-result URLs as `定向检索入口（非身份核验）`; never present them as verified personal or company profiles.
7. Resolve the consignee role before calling it a buyer. Exclude freight forwarders, NVOCCs, customs brokers, 3PL logistics hubs, port warehouses, FBA nodes, supplier self-consignees, and anonymous importers.
8. Do not say `最近六个月从中国采购` unless the legal buyer entity, transaction date within the 6-month window, China origin scope, stone product context, and openable trade evidence URL are supported.
9. Separate Mainland China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, and mixed/unknown origin. Do not collapse them into generic `China`.
10. Separate seller self-claims from independently verified certifications (ISO 9001, CE), ASTM test reports (ASTM C97, ASTM C170, ASTM C67), quarry ownership, and physical factory capacity.
11. Recheck every time-sensitive trade, company, person, email, phone, job posting, and procurement portal source on the run date.
12. Do not send outreach messages or contact prospects without explicit user authorization.

## Operating architecture

Run two deliberately separated passes:

1. **Research Execution (Codex / Discovery role):** understands Fujian Tianya Cultural Stone, builds ideal customer profiles (ICPs), discovers candidate companies, resolves legal entities and importing sites, gathers customs/trade evidence, identifies current procurement decision-makers, and structures the dataset.
2. **Sales Validation (Sales Intelligence role):** independently judges whether each company is worth developing now, diagnoses its stone procurement model (Direct Importer, Building Material Wholesaler, Hardscape Dealer, Project/Facade Contractor, Private Label OEM), identifies buying triggers, verifies supplier openness, assigns final R grades, and specifies the exact next sales action.

If only one agent or execution context is available, run the passes sequentially with independent instructions. Do not let the discovery pass assign its own final sales grade without a second review.

> Product relevance is not procurement fit. A company may import stone tiles or sell building materials but still be a poor near-term customer because its quarry contracts are locked, it buys exclusively through regional master distributors, its architectural specs require local quarry stone, its order volumes are below FCL container MOQ, or no supplier-change trigger exists.

## Phase 2 — Pilot 10 before scale gate

Never jump directly from an untested hypothesis to 50, 100, or 500 records.

### Pilot batch execution
1. Discover 10 representative candidate accounts in the target country/region.
2. Complete entity resolution, trade evidence extraction, procurement model diagnosis, decision-maker verification, and proposed outreach angle.
3. Submit the 10 accounts to the independent Sales Validation pass.
4. Document false positive patterns and adjust search terms, HS filtering, and disqualification criteria.

### Scale gate criteria
Scale to larger batches (25–50 accounts) only when the 10-account pilot satisfies:
- At least **6 of 10** are graded **R1 or R2**;
- At least **3 of 10** have strict **E1/E2** trade evidence or a validated project/expansion trigger;
- No more than **2 of 10** are hard exclusions (RX);
- The procurement decision-maker role and first-touch angle are clear for the majority of accounts.

If the scale gate fails, revise the market hypothesis or buyer archetype before expanding.

## Phase 3 — Research execution workflow

1. Map stone product synonyms, local trade terms (e.g. *ledger stone, stacked stone veneer, stone cladding, limestone pavers, split face slate, masonry supply*), HS codes, and target countries (North America, Western Europe, Middle East, Australia).
2. Discover candidate buyers from customs import databases, public bills of lading (BOL), trade association member directories (e.g. Natural Stone Institute, Building Stone Institute), architectural stone trade fair exhibitor/attendee lists (Coverings, Marmomac, Middle East Stone), and company import portals.
3. Resolve the legal importing entity, parent group, operating sites, and official domain (`domain.com`).
4. Classify the buyer role: Importer/Wholesaler, Masonry Dealer, Tile Showroom, Contractor/Developer, Landscape Specialist, Private Label OEM, or Excluded Logistics Node.
5. Verify China trade evidence (Mainland China / HK / Taiwan origin, run-date-minus-6-months window, stone product keywords).
6. Exclude freight forwarders, NVOCCs, customs brokers, 3PL warehouses, and unverified consignees.
7. Identify current procurement decision-makers: Purchasing Director, Procurement Manager, Stone Category Manager, VP Supply Chain, Chief Estimator, Project Sourcing Manager.
8. Verify public contact channels (LinkedIn, official corporate contacts, dedicated business emails) with source URL, purpose, and verification timestamp.

## Customs/trade evidence grades

- **E1 (Strict 6-month China-related customs proof):** Legal buyer resolved; exact shipment/sample date inside `run_date - 6 months` window; explicit Mainland China / HK / Taiwan origin; stone product description matching cultural stone/limestone/granite/ledger stone; openable evidence URL.
- **E2 (Credible but incomplete trade evidence):** Entity resolved with verified China stone import relationship, but missing exact date inside window, complete HS detail, or shipper name.
- **E3 (Trade/demand signal only):** Official supplier registration, stone tender notice, active hiring for stone buyer, or trade directory listing without individual shipment BOL row.
- **U0 (Unusable):** Unresolved entity, expired listing, or non-openable trade link.

## Contact grades

- **C1:** Verified current individual decision-maker with stone procurement, sourcing, category management, or project estimation authority.
- **C2:** Individual profile identified, but current title, employer, or site authority requires same-day confirmation.
- **C3:** General corporate executive or general sales route available for referral.
- **C0:** No public individual contact found.

## Sales validation & action grades (R-Grades)

- **R1 — Develop now:** High product/ICP fit, verified procurement model, reachable decision-maker, active buying trigger or China second-source opening, clear Tianya Stone proof asset.
- **R2 — Validate then develop:** Strong technical fit, but one key commercial factor (e.g. FCL volume capability, current quarry contract status, site vs. HQ purchasing authority) needs a single focused validation step.
- **R3 — Nurture/observe:** Relevant stone buyer, but current supply chain is locked, project timeline is distant, or trigger is weak.
- **RX — Exclude:** Hard disqualifier (logistics entity, retail consumer, machinery vendor, false entity, local quarry-only mandate).

## Sales scoring — 100-Point system

1. **Product / Application Fit (20 pts):** Aligns with Tianya's cultural stone veneer, ledger panels, natural limestone, or granite pavers.
2. **Procurement Model & Supplier Openness (20 pts):** Direct importer / stocking wholesaler with openness to China factory OEM supply.
3. **Customs & Trade Evidence (20 pts):** E1 (20), E2 (14), E3 (8), U0 (0).
4. **Current Trigger & Timing (15 pts):** New project spec, catalog refresh, cost-down pressure, spring stocking season, active hiring.
5. **Decision-Maker Reachability (15 pts):** C1 (15), C2 (10), C3 (5), C0 (0).
6. **Public Channel Quality (10 pts):** Verified corporate email / procurement route with exact purpose tag.

**Score Mapping:** A+ (90–100), A (82–89), B (72–81), C (below 72), RX (Excluded).
